SHIFT//RUSH background music
============================
Drag any song in here (.wav, .mp3, .ogg) and it plays in the menu and in races. No setup needed.
Import settings are applied for you (streamed from disk, compressed), so big files are fine.

Players choose in Settings > Audio > Music (also in the pause menu):
  SHUFFLE ALL  - every song here, in random order, cross-fading between them
  OFF          - silence
  <song name>  - that one song on repeat

The song's file name is its name in the menu: underscores and dashes become spaces.
Asset Store music must not go in the public repo: audio files in this folder are git-ignored.

Names and lines are drafts until approved.

## Race start (story races only)

1. Drone camera sweeps the event: balloon screen, crowd, track. The two masked hosts commentate.
2. Each racer's chopper flies in, in formation along the track, car hanging under it.
3. Drop button: the player drops whenever they like, but never past the start point (auto drop there). AI drops on its own.
4. Landing goes straight into the race. Camera cuts from the drone to the racers, a host says "Oh, they're here!"
5. When the event ends, the chopper scoops the player straight away.

Not every race has a police chase. City tracks (one or two) come after the story is done.

## Then gameplay

First race: beat one named rival to the line while the police join partway. Not a 1-2-3 race.
After that: the main loop. Earn fans and hype, improve your ride, climb the Channel 13 top 10.
